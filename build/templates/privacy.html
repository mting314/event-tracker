Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ generated_at|jst_date }}

LL Lottery Tracker (the “Service”) is a free, non-commercial hobby project that tracks Love Live! / seiyuu events and their lottery (抽選 / 先行) application rounds. It consists of this website, an optional “Log in with Discord” subscription manager, and a Discord bot that sends reminders. This policy explains what data the Service handles when you choose to log in or subscribe. If you only browse the public event pages, no account data is collected.

What we collect

  • Discord account identifiers. When you click “Log in with Discord,” we use Discord OAuth2 with the identify scope only. From that we receive your Discord user ID, username/display name, and avatar. We do not request or receive your email address, your servers/guilds, your messages, your friends, or any other Discord data.
  • Your subscriptions and reminder settings. The events and series you subscribe to, and your reminder preferences (lead times, whether to receive DMs), stored against your Discord user ID.
  • A login session cookie. A single cookie holding a signed token with your Discord user ID, display name, and avatar so you stay logged in. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and let you view and manage your subscriptions on the website.
  • To send you the reminders you asked for, via Discord direct message and/or a server channel you configured.

How it’s stored & shared

  • Data is stored in a private database on a server controlled by the maintainer. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • The only external service your data is sent to is Discord (to authenticate you and deliver reminders), governed by Discord’s Privacy Policy.
  • Event/lottery information shown on the site is public information curated from official and fan sources; it is not personal data about you.

Your choices & deletion

  • You can add or remove any subscription at any time — on the website or with the bot’s /unsubscribe command — which deletes that record.
  • Logging out clears your session cookie.
  • To have all of your data deleted, remove your subscriptions and/or contact the maintainer (below) and it will be erased from the database.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord’s own minimum age requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

This is a personal project. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/mting314/event-tracker.
